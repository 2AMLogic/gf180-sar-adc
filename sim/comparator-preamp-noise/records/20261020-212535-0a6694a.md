- **Noise methodology**: `ac-based` — ngspice `.noise` on the static preamplifier, about a real DC operating point, with the StrongARM latch instantiated in reset so its input capacitance loads the preamp. The reported input-referred noise is the total integrated OUTPUT noise (1 Hz .. 1 GHz) divided by the measured DC gain, NOT ngspice's own input-referred integral (see the inoise_band_uv check for why). This is a MEASUREMENT of the PDK's own device noise models, not a hand-built noise model propagated through a circuit. It is the dominant term because every downstream contribution is divided by the preamp gain. The residual latch term cannot be measured by `.noise` at all -- a reset-and-regenerate structure has no DC operating point -- so spec/comparator-budget-memo.md S8.1 bounds it analytically, and S8 states both the cost of the transient-noise Monte Carlo campaign this topology avoids and what would force it back.
- **Note**: NGSPICE UNITS, THE LOAD-BEARING DETAIL. ngspice's `onoise_total` / `inoise_total` are RMS VOLTS, not noise power -- verified independently against an ideal 1 kOhm resistor: 4.0693 nV/rtHz flat over 1 Hz .. 1 GHz gives onoise_total = 1.2868e-4, which is 4.0693e-9 * sqrt(1e9) = 128.68 uV RMS exactly, not its square. An earlier draft of this manifest took sqrt(onoise_total) and reported 21.8 mV rms of comparator noise -- 200x the true value and 40x over the budget ceiling. Nothing about that number looked syntactically wrong; it is exactly the class of plausible-but-invalid noise claim issue #9 was curated to prevent, and it is recorded here so the next reader does not repeat it.
- **Note**: INTEGRATED NOISE, NOT A DENSITY AT A BANDWIDTH. A comparator SAMPLES its input at the decision instant; it does not filter. So the quantity the budget needs is the total integrated noise at the preamp output, referred to the input by the DC gain -- which is what vn_in_uv is.
- **Note**: fnoicor is left at the PDK default (0, as-extracted). devchar S3.4 requires every flicker number in this repo to state its setting and measured the worst-case setting to be ~11x in power. Rather than re-run the whole grid at fnoicor = 1, this record reports the flicker FRACTION of the total, which is what a reader multiplies to get the worst-case answer.
- **Note**: The 10 fF/side wiring allowance on the preamp outputs is a placeholder for interconnect that does not exist before the floorplan (#16). It is deliberately small: more load means less noise bandwidth and LESS reported noise, so under-loading is the conservative direction and post-layout extraction (#17) can only improve this number.
