## Why this record exists

Items 1 (Design sources) and 2 (Layout) graded `unmet`/`no_evidence` in both
partitions although their artifacts exist, because since klayout-tools#2718 /
#2844 they accept only an artifact-anchored envelope, and item 1 only one
produced by `klt signoff --attest 1`. This repo had none.

**The pin bump is part of the change, not a side effect.** The old pin
predates both upstream changes, and no released wheel has `--attest` (0.7.0
does not), so the only way to grade these rows `met` is a commit of the
0.7.1 development line. Re-grading the *unchanged* previous manifest under
the new pin first moved **no row** (7/22 before and after; only
`source_doc_content_hash` and `grading_ruleset_id` changed), so the four
moves below are caused by the new citations alone.

## What this record does NOT assert

- That the inventories are exhaustive. The CDAC and track-switch schematics
  have no derived netlist or equivalence test (sources only), and
  `design/smoke_test.sch` is an environment smoke test, deliberately not
  listed.
- That the digital gate-level netlists are byte-reproducible: their freshness
  check is formal equivalence to the RTL. Byte reproduction is pinned to one
  Yosys build this host does not have.
- That `sar_ctrl.gds` reproduces from `pnr_sar_ctrl.py`: it was not re-run, so
  item 2 digital carries no regeneration claim.
- That CI re-runs the freshness commands. CI (no `klt`) re-hashes every
  inventoried file against `freshness.json`; the commands re-run under
  `--regen`.
- Anything about DRC/LVS/spec: items 3, 4 and 5 are unchanged (3.digital is
  `unmet`, 208 violations). The PDK re-pin (#514, #515) is unlanded; if it
  lands after this, #515's re-qualification must re-attest.
