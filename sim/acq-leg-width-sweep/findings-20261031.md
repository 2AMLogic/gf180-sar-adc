# Intermediate acquisition-leg widths: spectral half measured (schematic, 125 C)

Dated 2026-10-31. Issue #429. Supersedes the "spectral NOT measured" status of
[`findings-20261008.md`](findings-20261008.md) (not edited; its layout-cost
table in section 2 stands and is reused below). Investigation only: **no
ratified geometry, spec row, decision record or governing summary was edited**,
and no pass is claimed. This memo does not adopt, ratify or recommend adopting
any width.

## 1. Outcome

1. All six points (1.0, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2.068x) now have a fleet-run
   nine-point (3 process x 3 supply, 125 C) schematic FFT record. The x1.0
   control was measured on 2026-10-20 (`20261020-180420-c33f4e4`); the other
   five on 2026-10-31 (table, section 2). Earlier PDK-include blocker
   (2AMLogic/klayout-tools#2882, now closed) no longer reproduces with
   `klt 0.8.1.dev971` and `--runner-version-check warn`.
2. **The x1.0 control reproduces the DR-0019 endpoint**: worst SFDR 56.41 dB /
   composed ENOB 8.507 b at `ss_125c_2.97v`.
3. **Recovery is real but not smooth.** Worst-corner SFDR and ENOB rise with
   width up to 1.75x, then 2.068x is *worse* than 1.75x on SFDR (60.80 vs
   63.02 dB). Per-corner SFDR scatters by several dB between neighbouring
   widths (e.g. `ff_125c_2.97v`: 66.2, 62.4, 62.8, 67.2, 67.7, 70.2 dB), so the
   curve must not be interpolated or read as a smooth law; each point is one
   64-sample capture.
4. **The ratified Area row still fails at every width, including the 1x
   control** (0.151827 mm^2 against `< 0.1 mm^2`). Nothing here changes it.
5. Targets are not relaxed: ENOB `> 9.0`, SFDR `>= 62 dB`.

## 3. Reading

* **1.10x and 1.25x** (the widths that stay under the *proposed, unratified*
  `< 0.16 mm^2` of DR-0024): the worst corner still fails both ENOB (8.63 /
  8.88 b) and SFDR (56.9 / 59.6 dB). Little or no recovery at 1.10x; partial at
  1.25x. On this evidence neither meets the schematic hot-subset targets.
* **1.50x** is the smallest width whose schematic ENOB passes at all nine
  corners (9.041 b, margin 0.04 b), with one SFDR corner short (60.58 dB).
* **1.75x** is the only width passing both targets at all nine schematic
  corners, at +11.8 % block area (0.1698 mm^2, above the proposed 0.16 limit).
* **2.068x** (the rejected DR-0025 candidate) is not better than 1.75x on
  worst-corner SFDR in this data, at +16.9 % area.
* Do not infer a trend between points: neighbouring widths differ by several dB
  per corner, so the 1.75x vs 2.068x ordering may be capture-to-capture
  variation rather than physics.

## 4. Which candidates merit further work

Recommendation to the operator only; adoption, ratification and re-extraction
are separate decisions.

* **1.50x and 1.75x merit extracted, full-temperature (-40/27/125 C) and
  secondary-effect (charge injection, kickback, leakage, sampling-switch
  non-linearity) characterization before any decision**, because they are the
  only widths that recover the schematic hot-subset ENOB (1.5x) or both rows
  (1.75x). Both exceed the proposed `< 0.16 mm^2` area limit, so any adoption
  also needs the area decision (DR-0024) to go that way.
* **1.10x and 1.25x do not** merit that spend on this evidence; they remain
  FAIL on the worst schematic corner.
* A repeat capture (different seed/phase or more samples) at 1.5x, 1.75x and
  2.068x would be a cheap first step to separate real ordering from scatter.

## 5. What a reader must not conclude

* Schematic hot-subset recovery is **not** governing extracted performance. The
  governing rows in `sim/characterization-summary.md` (ENOB 8.855 b, SFDR
  60.41 dB, Area 0.151827 mm^2) all remain FAIL and are unchanged.
* The ratified Area row fails at every width including the control.
* Not covered: -40/27 C, extracted parasitics, other switch legs, the top-plate
  switch, secondary effects, Monte Carlo mismatch.

## 6. Tooling note

The fleet's log format prints per-sample values as `s000 = ...` instead of
`m_code_s000 = ...`, and post-#636 logs live in the artifact store.
`analyze_width_sweep.py` now accepts both sample formats and fetches stored logs
via `klt artifact get`; the shared `analyze_fft.py` is untouched, so older
sweeps are unaffected. No new klayout-tools friction was found: the earlier
PDK-include failure (#2882) is closed and did not recur.
