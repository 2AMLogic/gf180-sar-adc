## What moved, against R1

R1 asked for the right-side dead corridor at `x ≈ 437–605`, `y ≈ −12..214`,
and named two candidates: pull the comparator and its rail-stitch trunk
inward, or **re-derive the block's right-edge computation
(`max(bank_w, top.bbox().right) + 6 µm`) so the reached span matches the
routing that actually needs it**. The second candidate is what was taken,
and it turned out to apply to *two* corridors, not one:

| Corridor | Was | Now | What it actually has to reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| switch-cell `vdd`/`vss`/`vcm` bridge, far end (`far_x`) | 595.73 µm — `max(bank_w, top.bbox().right) + 6 µm`, i.e. past the comparator *and* past the comparator's own corridor | **443.86 µm** — one column pitch past the banks' own cross-bank stitch corridor | the decode banks' trunks (device rows end at `row_x1` = 437.36) |
| comparator `vdd`/`vss` strap | 587.93 / 589.43 µm — `top.bbox().right + 2.5 µm`, i.e. past the comparator | **477.00 / 477.70 µm** — inside `SWITCH_CMP_GAP` | a Comp-free column between the banks' trunks and the comparator's |

Both were *safe* before and are safe now: `geo.stitch` asserts the corridor
crosses neither `Comp` nor `Poly2` and would refuse to draw either of the new
columns if they were not clear. What changed is that neither is derived from
"whatever is furthest right at the moment this line runs" any more, so
neither can be pushed outward by a cell placed later.

**The block's right edge is now a drawn cell, not a corridor.** `ADC_BLOCK`
ends at 588.33 µm = `COMPARATOR`'s own right edge (585.43) + `GUARD_CLEARANCE`
(1.5) + `GUARD_RING_W` (1.4). Before this step the rightmost drawn geometry
in the block was a Poly2 stitch riser and a Metal1 trunk overhang, with
nothing but air between them and the comparator.

## The width floor this floorplan now sits against

The block's width is one chain, and after this change **every link in it is
either a drawn cell or a strap column that is provably needed**:

| From | To | µm | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| −10.70 | 0 | 10.70 | left guard ring + clearance + the arrays' own spine exit |
| 0 | 437.80 | 437.80 | the two CDAC arrays side by side (DR-0019 pitch, untouchable) |
| 437.80 | 442.80 | 5.00 | `REGION_GAP` × 2 |
| 442.80 | 472.70 | 29.90 | `ADC_TOP_SW`, the top-plate `V_cm` switch cell |
| 472.70 | 477.90 | 5.20 | **seven** top-level strap columns, all inside `SWITCH_CMP_GAP`: `topp`, `topn`, the bridge's three near columns, the comparator's `vdd`/`vss` |
| 477.90 | 478.70 | 0.80 | clearance to `COMPARATOR`'s own diffusion |
| 478.70 | 585.43 | 106.73 | `COMPARATOR` — transistor row (0..93.15) then the two folded load-resistor columns (..106.05) then the `vdd`/`vss` escape trunk (..106.73) |
| 585.43 | 588.33 | 2.90 | right guard clearance + ring |

The only slack left in that chain is the 0.80 µm at row 6, i.e. **the block
is within 0.8 µm of the floor its own strap-column plan imposes**. Getting
below it requires removing a strap column or moving the comparator, and that
is where R1 stops for a reason worth recording:

**The comparator cannot move left without lengthening `topp`/`topn`.** Its
left edge is pinned behind the `topp`/`topn` strap columns, which are pinned
behind the top-plate switch cell's own diffusion, which is pinned behind the
CDAC arrays. Every alternative placement examined during this pass — the
comparator stacked above the switch cell, the comparator in the corridor
right of the decode banks, the switch and comparator swapped — moves the
`topp`/`topn` strap to a column on the comparator's *right*, adding roughly
190–250 µm of Metal1 to the CDAC top plate. Under DR-0011 top-plate sampling
that net *is* the converter's summing node; ~10 fF of added `C_p` against
18.25 pF of array is a ~0.055 % gain-error term, small but real, and it is a
parasitics trade that belongs in its own decision record with its own PEX
re-run — not in a silent layout tweak filed as an area recovery. The
~24,800 µm² still empty in the right column is therefore **named, measured
and attributed**, not un-noticed.

## Judgment

Overall utilization is **0.4146**, inside the economy-review rubric's
0.40–0.65 band for a mixed-signal block (the review baseline's 0.3516 sat
below the floor and is what triggered the walk that produced R1 and R2).
`COMPARATOR`'s own local utilization is **0.2912**, clear of the 0.25
"matched pairs" red-flag floor the baseline's finding 5 measured it against.
Both named revision targets moved, by the amounts tabulated above, without
touching the CDAC arrays, the ratified `C_u`, the decode banks'
`comp.space.1`-floored pitch, or the DR-0010 reserve.

**This record does not issue a verdict.** It is the "quantify the recovery"
half of issue #215; whether 0.15054 mm² is the number to ratify is #198's
decision, and a fresh `economy-review` verdict — if one is wanted on the new
geometry — is its own pass.

## What was NOT re-measured or re-reviewed

- **Findings 1–3 of the review baseline** (CDAC arrays, decode banks,
  SAR-logic reserve) — carried forward unchanged; the per-cell table above
  shows the arrays' numbers are unchanged to the digit, and the banks' only
  change is the removal of a routing stub from their bbox.
- **Sub-die floorplan alternatives** (relocating the comparator, reshaping
  the 32 × 16 array, moving the SAR-logic reserve out from under the block)
  — still out of scope, as both predecessor records recorded, and now with
  the parasitic reason spelled out under "the width floor" above.
- **Parasitics.** No `topp`/`topn` route changed length in this step (the
  `topp`/`topn` strap columns are at exactly the X they were), so the PEX
  records under `parasitics/` are not invalidated by it. The `vdd`/`vss`
  rails got *shorter*, which can only help; that improvement is not
  quantified here because no testbench in this repo currently gates on
  supply-rail R.
