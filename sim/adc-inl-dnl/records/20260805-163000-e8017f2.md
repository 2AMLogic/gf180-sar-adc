    (−40/27/125 °C, ±10 % supply, process corners).
- **Statistical convention**: N/A (corner-matrix claim, not a distribution
  claim). Mismatch is off (harness default); the statistical half of the gain
  row is `sim/mc-cdac-mismatch/`'s job, and the #14 extracted Monte Carlo
  re-run is deferred.
- **Subset-corner justification**: this record measures ONLY `gain_err_lsb`,
  not the full 18-transition INL/DNL set, so it runs the same 2 endpoint
  transitions (1, 1023) per point that the schematic bench extrapolates
  `gain_err_lsb` from -- a deliberately narrow first extracted-netlist spec-line
  quantity (25.3 s/point, 45 points ≈ 19 min wall), chosen because the full
  18-transition sweep on this ~1300-device RC-laden extracted netlist is the
  "material multi-hour campaign" `layout/adc-top/parasitics/README.md`'s Compute
  note warns about. The remaining transitions (and the ENOB/power/Monte-Carlo
  benches) are deferred, not silently skipped -- see the follow-up issue.
- **Methodology**: `endpoint-extrapolation`. For each corner, a full
  transistor-level SAR conversion is run at input transition 1 and at
  transition 1023 through the extracted core; the input-referred error at each
  (`e1_lsb`, `e1023_lsb`, in LSB, read off the ideal shadow DAC) is measured at
  the trial that resolves that transition, and
  `gain_err_lsb = (e1023 - e1) × 1023 / (1023 - 1)` -- identical to
  `sim/adc-inl-dnl/`'s `inl_manifest()`. Same 2-endpoint pair, same formula, so
  the number is directly comparable to the schematic bench's own `gain_err_lsb`
  column.

## Result -- schematic vs extracted vs delta (27 comparable corners)

negative than schematic across every one of the 27 comparable corners: the
delta ranges **-0.5146 LSB** (`ff_-40c_3.63v`) to **-0.6340 LSB**
(`ss_125c_2.97v`), **mean -0.5552 LSB**. This is the extracted layout's
top-plate / interconnect parasitic-capacitance contribution to full-scale gain
attenuation -- the physical effect #53's top-plate-C_par finding predicts --

extracted `gain_err_lsb` of ≈ **-2.52 to -2.63 LSB**.
