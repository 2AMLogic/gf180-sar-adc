## Why this record exists

**The document behind T1 item 8 changed again.** PR #387 (issue #379) rewrote
`README.md#target-specification`'s **Supply** row after the `93ddfe3` full
re-read this envelope rests on, to carry
[DR-0036](../../spec/decision-records/DR-0036-vdd-decoupling-budget.md)'s
external decoupling figure (`≥ 40 nF` at the `V_DD` pin), its source-impedance
budget (`≤ 3 Ω` in the switching band, `Z_vdd ≈ ≤ 1.6 Ω` at the 2 MS/s stretch)
and a required-but-unsized on-die local decoupling term inside `ADC_BLOCK`.
Issue #381 declared that gap in the wrapped document's own Freshness section
rather than closing it, because reconciling it is DR-0036's work and not a
parasitics re-extraction's. Issue #389 closes it: the `Supply` row now states
all three clauses, and the unsized on-die term is carried in the document's
"What is not yet measured" list rather than silently dropped.

`signoff/run_signoff.py --check` re-hashes the wrapped document on every pull
request and fails loudly on exactly that, which is why this is a re-mint and
not an edit.

## What this record does NOT assert

- **Not a new full re-read of `sim/characterization-summary.md`.** Both item-8
  envelopes keep `assertion.reread_commit: 93ddfe3` (2026-09-21) and gained a
  third `assertion.partial_updates[]` entry — beside #381's and #358's —
  naming exactly what #389 reconciled and what it did not. Bumping the re-read
  stamp without redoing the row-by-row re-read against `README.md` would be
  the defect that document's own §"Currency defects" item 2 records.
- **Not that DR-0036 is ratified.** Its own status line reads *"proposed —
  requires operator sign-off"*, and `README.md`'s Supply row embeds that
  caveat inline. The reconciled row reports **against** the proposed budget
  while saying it is unratified — the same footing the `Area` row reports
  DR-0024's proposed `< 0.16 mm²` on. Nothing here is operator sign-off.
- **Not that the `V_DD` decoupling budget has been verified.** It has **no
  measured verdict**, and the row says so with the reason: the budget's
  *input* is measured (`sim/adc-rail-current/`'s 34.383980 mA `i_vdd_peak_ua`
  at `ff_-40c_3.63v`, 99.75 % of it the CDAC driver branch, against a
  39.5421 µA window average in the same corner — a factor of 869), but every
  ADC-level deck models the three supply islands as ideal DC sources
  (`tb_adc_power.spice`: `vddc`/`vddd`/`vddt` are `dc {vdd_val}`), so what the
  `≥ 40 nF` / `≤ 3 Ω` budget buys — and what missing it costs — is unmeasured.
  Filed as **#393**, the `V_CM` row's #358 done for this rail.
- **Not that the on-die `ADC_BLOCK` decoupling term is met or sized.** It is
  required by DR-0036 clause 3, **unmet in the block as drawn**, and
  deliberately unsized: `C_local ≥ L·I_pk²/(2·ΔV_inst²)` needs a package loop
  inductance (no package model exists) and a ratified instantaneous-excursion
  allowance (none exists), and the record's own sensitivity table spans
  5.43 pF to 228 nF. It is reported in the document's "What is not yet
  measured" list, which this envelope's `not_asserted` already names as part
  of the claim rather than an appendix to it.
- **Not that DR-0036's derivation is current against the geometry.** Step 8's
  523 Ω landing-site-to-decode-bank effective resistance was read off
  `layout/power/records/20260923-010407-d84c7b4.md`, **before** #378 / PR #388
  moved `ADC_BLOCK`'s block-level straps to Metal2; the same
  `peak-upper-bound.COMPARATOR` case now reads **4.93 V** instead of 17.99 V
  in the governing `20260923-084734-5f13cf9` (`layout/power/README.md` states
  both figures). DR-0036's own Consequences require whichever of #378 / that
  record is settled second to re-read the other; #388 landed second and did
  not. Flagged in the wrapped document and in both envelopes, not resolved
  here — this is a documentation pass, and re-deriving a decision record's
  step is that record's own work.
- **No manifest row was re-pointed, no `klt` pin moved, and no ratified bound,
  target or `tb.json` check was touched.** The only manifest edit is the
