- **Note**: THE TARGET SEARCH STARTS AT 71 ns, AND THAT IS NOT A CONVENIENCE. At each strobe both StrongARM outputs fall together for a few tens of picoseconds before they separate, so both isolation inverters pulse HIGH and the NOR SR latch briefly sees both inputs asserted -- its two outputs dip, and as they recover, dout can cross V_DD/2 transiently before losing. Without a search window the delay measurement latches onto that glitch at the FIRST strobe and reports a delay of about -62 ns (the first strobe minus the second), which is what record 20260801-035910-90d7e67 did at 6 of 45 corners. The glitch is real circuit behaviour, not a numerical artefact, and it is harmless: dout is read at 70 ns and at 130 ns at every corner and always holds the correct decision (that is what the dout_*_mid / dout_*_end checks are). What was wrong was the measurement, not the comparator.
