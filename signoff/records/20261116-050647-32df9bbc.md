## Why this record exists

PR #842 (issue #839) snapped `adc_top.gds`'s guard-ring geometry onto the
5 nm manufacturing grid. The parasitics manifest had been re-pointed at the
snapped stream without re-deriving what it asserts — the Judge blocked on
exactly that — so the Doctor pass re-ran
`layout/adc-top/parasitics/run_extract_parasitics.py` under the layout pin
(`klayout-tools==0.6.0`): `--check` failed on the two magnitude assertions
(`total_resistance_ohm` 128351.4673 -> 128351.4666, `total_capacitance_ff`
6149.35745 -> 6149.35685; `r_count`/`c_count` unchanged), `--regen-manifest`
re-baselined them, and the append-only extraction record
[`20261116-045110-d865283`](../adc-top/parasitics/records/20261116-045110-d865283.md)
was minted over the new report directory.

Five committed extracted decks
(`gen_extracted_{inl_dnl,enob_fft,power,switch_ron,dr0014_sampling}_tb.py`
re-derive from the *latest* `parasitics/reports/` record) went stale the
moment that record landed — `sim/tests/test_extracted_decks_current` caught
it in CI — so they were regenerated with their own generators. The diff is
the per-net lumped R/C values moving by ~1e-8 relative (the guard-ring
snap's delta); `timing_budget`'s deck already matched and was left alone.

Item 9 attests those decks' bytes, so its envelope had to move with them —

whenever a file an inventory names has changed, even though the inventory
