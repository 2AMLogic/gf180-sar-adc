# Intermediate acquisition-leg widths: layout cost measured, spectral recovery NOT measured

Dated 2026-10-08. Issue #429. Investigation only: **no ratified geometry, spec
row, decision record or governing summary was edited**, and no pass is claimed.
This memo does not adopt, ratify or recommend adopting any width.

DR-0025 declined the 2.068x acquisition-leg widening for its layout cost and
explicitly left every width between 1x and 2.068x unmeasured. This sweep
measures the *physical* half of that gap and records, precisely, why the
*spectral* half could not be taken in this session.

## 1. Outcome in four lines

1. **Layout is feasible at every swept width.** All six points (1.0, 1.1, 1.25,
   1.5, 1.75, 2.068x) draw with the unmodified `layout/adc-top/gen_adc_top.py`
   `build()`; `adc_top` and `adc_block` are DRC-clean and LVS-match under the
   repo-pinned `klayout-tools==0.6.0`, same 296 / 323 transistors and 1024
   unit caps as production.
2. **Area cost is linear in this layout and not small.** The block grows by
   +4.0 um of height per +0.1x of scale (599.35 um wide, fixed), i.e.
   **+2,397 um^2 (+1.58 % of 151,827 um^2) per +0.1x**. The measured points show
   exactly this slope; it is a property of this placer (the decode-bank row
   height tracks the widened `Xsi` island), not an assumption.
3. **The ratified Area row fails at every point, including the control.**
   `< 0.1 mm^2` is already failed by the 1x production block (0.151827 mm^2).
   Against DR-0024's *proposed, unratified* `< 0.16 mm^2`, the points at or
   below 1.25x fit (0.15782 mm^2) and the points at or above 1.5x do not
   (0.16381 mm^2). That is information about a pending decision, not a pass.
4. **No SFDR / ENOB number was produced for any intermediate point.** The batch
   fleet could not run the 125 C nine-point grid (section 4). No schematic
   hot-subset recovery is claimed, and none is inferred or interpolated. The
   question "does any intermediate width merit further characterization?"
   therefore stays **open**; only its cost axis is now known.

Controls and cross-checks:

* **The regenerated 1x control is byte-identical to production**:
  `acq-leg-1x/adc_top.gds` and `adc_block.gds` `cmp`-equal
  `layout/adc-top/adc_top.gds` / `adc_block.gds`, and the `areas_um2` tally
  equals `layout/adc-top/area.json` field for field.
* **Historical 2.068x record is not reproduced bit-for-bit, by design.**
  `acq-leg-2.068x/` (0.176127 mm^2, 599.03 x 294.02 um, issue #248) predates
  the layout changes since (#223, #384, #388); re-drawing 2.068x on the
  current tree (`acq-leg-2.068x-regen/`, 0.177432 mm^2) is the same-basis
  comparison point. The historical directory was not overwritten.
* Tool provenance: DRC/extract/LVS used `uvx --from klayout-tools==0.6.0 klt`
  (the `layout/toolchain.json` pin; klayout engine resolved 0.30.12, which
  `klt` itself warns differs from its tested 0.30.10 -- verdicts are
  unaffected, report counts may differ). Production `adc_block.gds` under that
  same pinned `klt` is clean / match, which is why it is the reference.
  The host's installed `klt 0.7.0` reports 2,336 `contact.width.1` violations
  and an LVS mismatch on the **production** block and on all six points
  identically (a deck/extractor change since the pin); those runs were
  discarded rather than recorded, and no candidate differed from the control
  under either tool version.
* Raw `klt` JSON: `layout/adc-top/candidates/acq-leg-<S>x/reports/`.

### Context already on record (different basis; not this sweep's numbers)

`sim/dr0019-cu-sweep-findings.md` measured the two endpoints at schematic
level, 125 C subset, at an older netlist vintage: 1x worst SFDR 56.41 dB /
composed ENOB 8.507 bits; 2.068x 60.80 dB / 9.170 bits. The governing
**extracted** rows in `sim/characterization-summary.md` (ENOB 8.855 bits, SFDR
60.41 dB, Area 0.151827 mm^2) all FAIL and are unchanged by this work.
Schematic hot-subset recovery is not governing extracted performance, and the
intermediate widths have neither.

## 4. What this does and does not let a reader conclude

* Cost axis: known, linear, and (relative to the *ratified* row) irrelevant to
  pass/fail -- Area fails at 1x already. It matters only against the
  unratified `< 0.16 mm^2` proposal, which 1.5x and up exceed.
* Benefit axis: unknown. Whether 1.25x (the largest width that stays under
  `< 0.16 mm^2`) recovers a useful fraction of the 2.068x gain cannot be said;
  the earlier 2.068x result cannot be scaled down linearly or otherwise.
* So the answer to "does any candidate merit subsequent extracted /
  full-temperature / secondary-effect characterization" is **deferred**: if the
  fleet becomes usable, the cheapest informative next step is the six-point
  schematic grid; points at or below 1.25x are the only ones that could ever be
  consistent with the proposed `< 0.16 mm^2`. Adoption, ratification and any
  production re-extraction are separate decisions and are not made here.
* Not covered: the top-plate switch scaling, the other three legs, kickback /
  charge injection / leakage / secondary effects, extracted parasitics.
