- **Note**: THE PREAMP IS THE MITIGATION, AND THIS IS THE MEASUREMENT THAT SAYS SO. DR-0007 chose a static preamplifier ahead of the StrongARM latch partly because it makes the input stage unidirectional: the regenerating nodes, which swing the full 3.3 V rail, are two stages away from the CDAC top plate instead of being capacitively coupled to it through the latch input pair's C_gd. This record is the evidence for that claim, not an assertion of it -- a bare StrongARM's kickback at the same devices would be visible in the same measurement.
- **Note**: THE TOP PLATE IS FLOATING, DELIBERATELY. Biasing the inputs from ideal voltage sources would restore the injected charge instantly and report ~0 kickback no matter how bad it was; that is the standard way this measurement is faked. Here the input is 18.254 pF (spec/cdac-sizing-memo.md S5.2's C_side, 512 x 35.6528 fF, the array #202 physically implemented) fed through 1 Gohm, so the RC is ~18 ms against a 62.5 ns bit cycle -- the bias network sets the operating point and then does nothing. C_par (the comparator input plus the adc_tp_sw off-state load that DR-0014 adds to the same node, 57-160 fF per sim/top-plate-cpar/records/20261105-061018-e5489fc.md) is <= 0.9 % of the node and is deliberately not modelled: its omission makes the measured residual conservative by that same <= 0.9 %, and its <= 1.0 % input-referral factor 1/k is carried by the memo, not the deck.
- **Note**: DR-0014 BOTTOM-PLATE SAMPLING IS THE PREMISE, NOT DR-0006. Re-derived and re-measured by issue #833: the input is sampled on the bottom plates and the per-side top-plate V_cm switch (adc_tp_sw) opens at the edge into ph3, one bit cycle before the bottom plates leave V_in. During every bit trial the top plate is floating about V_cm (the deck's fixed-V_cm excitation), and the residue a trial presents spans +/-1.65 V differential at trial 1 down to +/-half-LSB -- which is why the deck now samples three residue points and the signal-dependent metric is the spread across them. The excitation's shape (a real comparator instance driving a floating, bias-observed top plate) is unchanged and was traced, not asserted, by DR-0025 item 3: kickback originates entirely inside the comparator subckt with no path through a CDAC switch, and the sampling instant is defined solely by adc_tp_sw opening. The superseded records (20260801-042959-dbb3ab5, 20260825-044912-9fe3b68, 20261020-180419-bc92f79) ran this deck's earlier top-plate-sampling premise at ctop = 8.83 pF with no third residue point.
- **Note**: SAMPLING-SWITCH AND BOTTOM-PLATE DYNAMICS ARE NOT MODELLED HERE. The top plate is a lumped capacitance, not the real 512-position array with its switch resistances; sim/cdac-bit-settling/ (issue #8) owns that. Combining them would confound kickback with settling. The lumped model is the conservative direction for the residual kick (no path to ground at all) and the optimistic one for the peak excursion (no series resistance), which is why the peak is reported separately rather than folded into the claim.
- **Note**: MISMATCH IS OFF (PDK default sw_stat_mismatch = 0). Kickback and offset are separable effects and are budgeted separately; a mismatched instance would add its offset to the measured top-plate displacement and make neither number attributable.
- **Note**: NUMERICAL RESOLUTION IS PART OF THIS MEASUREMENT, AND IT IS ~1 uV -- NOT 1 nV. The residual kickback this topology produces is SMALLER THAN THE RESOLUTION OF THE TOOL THAT REPORTS IT, so every residual figure from this deck is a resolution-limited BOUND, not a point value. The floor is ~1 uV and it is set by ngspice's `meas` RESULT precision (~6 significant digits), which on the ~1.7 V top-plate levels this deck differences quantizes each level to ~1 uV before the `measure` expressions subtract them. The corner logs show it directly (bp0 = 1.70000e+00, bp1 = 1.70001e+00), and it is why every completed point reports kick_diff_*, kick_sigdep_uv and peak_dip_uv as exact integer uV. The tightened tolerances above (vntol = 1e-9, reltol = 1e-4) DO reach the deck (sim/harness/runner.py, covered by sim/tests/test_harness.py) and they buy solver accuracy, but they do NOT move this floor: an earlier version of this note claimed they pushed the numerical floor three decades below the reported values, and that claim was wrong. Keep them for comparability -- a re-run that drops them is not comparable with this record -- but read kick_diff_small_uv / kick_sigdep_uv as '<= N x 1 uV', and read kick_sigdep_lsb's trailing digits as arithmetic on a quantized input rather than as resolved precision. Buying the extra decades requires measuring a quantity that is already small (a behavioural differential node referenced near 0 V), not tighter tolerances. General rule: sim/harness/README.md, 'The `meas` result-precision floor'.
