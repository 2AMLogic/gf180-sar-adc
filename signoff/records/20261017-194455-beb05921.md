## Why this record exists

Two records were minted from the same parent,
[`20261015-222800-a90cb5ba`](20261015-222800-a90cb5ba.md), on separate
branches, so the `Supersedes` chain forked:

- [`20261017-193508-9869b691`](20261017-193508-9869b691.md) (#559, merged to
  `main`) cites the `sar_ctrl` gate-level LVS and a `klt erc` supply run:
  4.digital and 11.digital `unmet` -> `met`, 9/22, under the old grader pin.
- [`20261017-193831-9869b691`](20261017-193831-9869b691.md) (#560) bumps the
  grader pin and attests T1 items 1 and 2 for both partitions: 11/22.

Neither report describes the merged manifest, and a grader report is not
hand-mergeable (it is hashed into `freshness.json`). So the merged tree was
re-graded with `run_signoff.py --regen` and this record supersedes both. Both
predecessors are kept unedited, as the append-only rule requires.
