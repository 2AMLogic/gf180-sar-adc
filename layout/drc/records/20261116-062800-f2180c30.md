## The fix that landed: SB.2, resistor-locally

`place._RES_COLUMN_GAP` is a new resistor-local constant (550 nm), consumed
by BOTH `resistor_column_width()` and `ResistorColumn.width` — the same
one-expression discipline issue #118 installed after the terminal-lead
crowding. `geometry.COLUMN_GAP` itself is untouched: it is shared repo-wide
with import-time asserts, and #838's Out of Scope excludes it. The drawn
SAB-to-SAB gap between the two resistors' markers IS this column gap (the
markers stay body-sized), so 550 clears SB.2's 420 nm with 130 nm of
headroom.

Why 550 and not the discipline's native 420 + 120 = 540: the gap value
propagates into `gen_adc_top`'s block-level arithmetic (the comparator's
escape reservation `load_resistor_span` → the supply straps, the SAR-logic
reserve, the guard-ring tap arrays), and that arithmetic is not
5 nm-grid-preserving under arbitrary deltas. **Measured sweep** (0.8.0 deck,
`adc_block`, generators re-run per candidate):

| `_RES_COLUMN_GAP` | Δ vs 400 | `*.ongrid.1` on `adc_block` |
|---|---|---|
| 550 | +150 | **0** |
| 600 | +200 | 3708 |
| 700 | +300 | **0** |
| 800 | +400 | 3708 |
| 1000 | +600 | **0** |

At 600 and 800 the off-grid markers are the `contact`/`metal1`/`comp`/
`pplus` families over the SAR-logic reserve and the guard rings — the same
defect family #839 owns (the `// 2` centring computations flagged in
#840's record), re-deriving from a width this issue moved. Folding #839's
fix in is #838's own Out of Scope, so the headroom is rounded to the
nearest grid-clean delta instead: 550. (550 also keeps the drawn
Poly2-to-Poly2 column clearance at 550 nm, 2.3x `poly2.space.1`'s 240.)

## The half that is routed, not taken: SB.10 is incompatible with the extraction

SB.10 (`poly2.enclosing.sab.1`, 220 nm minimum Poly2 extension beyond
related SAB) is an **enclosure** rule: deck-legal means
`poly2 ⊇ dilate(sab, 220 nm)` on every SAB edge. The recognised body is
`poly2.and(sab).and(res_mk).and(resistor)`; the deck's terminal ("C")
region is "Poly2 minus the recognised body" (quoted verbatim from the deck
source, `klayout_tools/decks/gf180mcu.py`, BOTH at the pinned 0.6.0 build
and at 0.8.0). Three consequences, each verified:

1. **The marked region must stay exactly the drawn serpentine.** The
   extracted identity is `R = sheet_rho · area / port_width²` with the
   netlist's `L = area / port_width` and `W = port_width`; the committed
   `comparator.spice` pins `150000 ppolyf_u_1k L=150U W=1U` byte-for-byte on
   every plain `run_lvs.py` run. The marked region therefore needs exactly
   area `1.5e8 nm²`, one polygon, and two full-width (1000 nm) port
   junctions — and since `poly2 = body` has exactly that area, any marked
   region strictly smaller than the body cannot reach it. So `sab ⊇ body`.
2. **The halo band is then forced and it is unmarked.** With
   `sab ⊇ body`, SB.10 forces `poly2 ⊇ dilate(body, 220)`, whose excess over
   the body is a connected band surrounding the body on all sides and
   touching it along its whole perimeter. For every ring point, at least
   one of the three marker layers must miss it (or it would be part of the
   recognised body and grow its area) — so the band is unmarked Poly2 and
   no deck-legal shape choice (which layer carries it, how large it is)
   removes it.
3. **One unmarked band ⇒ one contact region ⇒ no device.** Measured, on the
   real cell, with the halo actually drawn (`_RES_FOLD_SPACE`/column gap
   widened to the SB.10-forced 680 nm minimum, `legs = 4` re-derived so the
   area identity still holds exactly):

   | | committed (no halo) | halo drawn |
   |---|---|---|
   | marked polygons | 2 | 2 (serpentines, bbox 5800 x 37050 -> 6400 x 36900 at the widened fold) |
   | marked area | 3.0e8 nm² | 3.0e8 nm² (unchanged — `2 × 150000 × 1000`) |
   | contact components touching each wire | **2** (one per lead) | **1** (band + both leads merged) |
   | `klt extract` device count | 29 | **27** |
   | `ppolyf_u_1k` devices | 2 at `150000 … L=150U W=1U` | **0** (`pop`/`pon` merge) |

   The extractor needs two contacts attached at the ends of the wire; with
   one contact component touching everywhere, it forms neither — and this
   holds at BOTH deck pins (same derivation). An SB.10-compliant,
   extraction-clean poly resistor does not exist at the pinned spec.

Per #838's scope guard this is routed, not worked around: the alternatives
each spend something ratified — re-baselining the extracted value
(forbidden here), changing the deck's terminal derivation upstream
(klayout-tools, #652's pin), moving the loads to a different device class,
or drawing SAB-inset geometry at a re-baselined W. None of these is this
issue's to take. The friction-protocol issue for the deck side is filed
generically (see the PR referencing this record).
