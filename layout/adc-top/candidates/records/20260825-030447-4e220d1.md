**Read**: the entire area cost is a **height-only** growth of the decode
bank row, propagated through the guard ring. Width (X) is untouched on every
field, because `place.column_pitch(l)` depends only on gate length `l`
(unchanged: `l=0.28u` throughout) — the candidate widens the T-gate's
*channel width*, which `geometry.draw_mosfet` draws along +Y, not the
column pitch. Every OTHER region — the CDAC capacitor arrays themselves,
the comparator, the top-plate switches (not widened by this candidate —
`adc_tp_sw` is untouched), and the reserved SAR-logic region — is
byte-identical to the ratified tally, which is the expected, checkable
signature of an isolated single-leg width change reaching a
width-parametric placer.

Mechanism: all four legs of every one of the bank's 9 weighted CDAC cells
(`Xsi`/`Xsr`/`Xsh`/`Xsl`, 36 T-gates total per side) share ONE row
(`place.draw_devices`, one call per bank), bottom-aligned at a common
`row_y0`. Only `Xsi`'s NMOS/PMOS actives grew (10u→20.68u NMOS, 20u→41.36u
PMOS); the bank row's Y extent is set by the TALLEST active in the row,
which was previously the ratified PMOS at 20u and is now the widened `Xsi`
PMOS at 41.36u — hence the ~70 % per-bank-row height growth, diluted to
+17.00 % once amortised over the whole block (CDAC arrays, comparator,
SAR-logic reserve unaffected).

### Against both governing figures (#237)

| Target | Value | Ratified (0.150536 mm²) | Candidate (0.176127 mm²) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| README.md line 98, still-nominally-ratified | `< 0.1 mm²` | **+50.5 % over** (already failing) | **+76.1 % over** |
| DR-0024, `proposed` — **not ratified** (#237 open) | `< 0.16 mm²` | −5.9 % (passes, if ratified) | **+10.1 % over** (fails even the relaxed target) |

**Both rows already fail at the ratified geometry** (#237's own Context: the
as-built 0.150536 mm² exceeds the `< 0.1 mm²` row DR-0024 was proposed to
relax; DR-0024 itself is not yet ratified). The candidate acquisition-leg
width makes the miss worse against **either** figure — it does not merely
fail to help, it costs an additional 17.0 % of the already-overrun block,
and pushes the block **out of range of the relaxed DR-0024 target too**
(0.176127 mm² vs. `< 0.16 mm²`, +10.1 % over). Widening the acquisition leg
to recover the ENOB/SFDR governing FAILs (#238's premise,
`sim/dr0019-cu-sweep-findings.md` Sec 0 item 3) would trade a dynamic-range
recovery for an area regression that fails under either candidate reading of
the Area row — this is exactly the kind of consequence #238's own text
warned the recovery "must not be read as achievable margin until [the five
deferred quantities] are measured," and this is the measurement for area.

## What this does and does not decide

**Nothing here changes a ratified number, and nothing here re-decides
DR-0019, DR-0024, or #237's ratification question.** Per `CLAUDE.md`, no
ratified spec value is relaxed to make this row pass — the Area row already
fails against the still-ratified `< 0.1 mm²` target before this candidate is
applied at all, and that fact is reported, not worked around. This record
measures a term (#238's own framing); it does not decide whether the
acquisition-leg-widening trade is adopted, and it does not resolve #237.
