### PDN, and why the `fit` attempt drops the upper-metal mesh

The `reference` attempt's PDN follows this platform's own
`pdn_grid_strategy_7t_6M.cfg`: `Metal1` row rails plus `Metal4`/`Metal5`
straps. The `fit` attempt, run first against that same PDN, failed outright
in the `floorplan` stage before placement was even attempted
(`PDN-0185: Insufficient width (31.36 um) to add straps on layer Metal5 ...
total strap width 49.3 um and offset 44.8 um`) — the upper-metal mesh is
sized for macros far larger than this reserved region's 40 um height, so
that failure is a PDN-config mismatch, not a finding about whether the
*netlist* fits. The `fit` attempt's PDN was changed to row rails
(`Metal1`/`followpins`) only — the floor every standard-cell macro needs
regardless of size, and consistent with a macro this small being expected to
have its `vdd`/`vss` pins stitched into `layout/adc-top/`'s own upper-metal
rails at a later composition step (out of scope here; not attempted). The
`reference` attempt's PDN was left as originally drafted (full mesh),
since its box is large enough to carry it. This is a difference in each
attempt's *own* PDN configuration, not a difference in the reserved
footprint or the netlist.
