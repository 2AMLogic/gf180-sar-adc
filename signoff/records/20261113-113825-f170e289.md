## Why this record exists

with **no `ok` point supersedes nothing** — `run_corners.py` now withholds a
requested `--supersedes` pointer when `points_ok` is 0 — and names the one
record that predates the rule. `sim/README.md` is a pinned, hash-bound input of
the T1 item 10 (repo hygiene) evidence, so that paragraph moved its sha256 from

recorded hash moves no pin, and the pointer re-point below leaves it stale
again by construction, exactly as at every previous regen.
