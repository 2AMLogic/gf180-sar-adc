## Spent vs lower bound, per sub-block

Lower bounds are stated with their basis. None is a claim that the bound is
reachable; each one says what the sub-block would cost at zero waste under the
stated assumption.

### CDAC arrays — 49,339.2 µm²

| Measure | µm² | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Plate area only (1,024 × 16.0) | 16,384.0 | measured (MiM layer 117/5 area in `ADC_BLOCK`) and the DR-0019 4.0 µm plate, a **fixed input** |
| Tile floor (1,024 × 6.4²) | 41,943.0 | repo-stated pitch: plate + 2 × 0.6 (MIMTM.3) + 1.2 (MIMTM.1); this record did not re-derive the rules |
| Spent | 49,339.2 | measured |

Gap, plate → tile floor: 25,559 µm². The plate is 39.1 % of the tile. This is
the largest single "cost of the rules" in the block, 16.8 % of it, and no
placement change touches it.

Gap, tile floor → spent: 7,396.2 µm² (15.0 % of the arrays). Each array bbox is
216.4 × 114.0 µm against a 204.8 × 102.4 µm grid, 11.6 µm larger in each
direction. Inferred reason: dummy ring and spine exits (from the
`gen_adc_top.py` comments); not checked at cell level.

### Decode banks — 30,139.2 µm²

| Measure | µm² | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Drawn area (zero-whitespace bound for this cell design) | 16,721.2 | measured |
| Row only (2 × 440.94 × 31.5) | 27,780.4 | repo-stated row width (`area.json`) × measured height |
| Spent | 30,139.2 | measured |

Gap to drawn: 13,418 µm² (44.5 % of the banks). Gap to row-only: 2,358.8 µm²,

Transistor channel area is not the cost: all 323 transistors have a total

including taps and rings. A rule-derived row/height floor for a 31.5 µm tall
bank (the largest device W is 20 µm) was **not** derived; it is unverified
whether the height can shrink without folding.

### Comparator — 5,304.9 µm²

Drawn 1,594.8 µm²; gap to drawn 3,710.1 µm². Reasons already on record
(`40cfeb8`): two folded load-resistor columns and the `vdd`/`vss` trunk set the
width, and the placement is pinned behind the `topp`/`topn` strap columns.

### Top-plate switch cell — 916.3 µm²

Drawn 449.1 µm². 0.6 % of the block; not a lever.

### Not attributable to any sub-block

The SAR band (37,321.5) and the empty core (28,909.2) together are 66,230.7 µm²
(43.6 % of the block). The sub-blocks' own whitespace (arrays 9,754, banks
13,418, comparator 3,710, switch 467) adds 27,349 µm² that is already inside the
instance bboxes.

## Findings

**F1 — the "irreducible packing floor" is a property of the stacked topology,
not of the rules.** `area_feasibility.py` reports a floor of
440.94 × 253.32 = 111,699 µm² (committed output
`reports/…/area_feasibility.txt`). Its height includes the 62.27 µm SAR band
that is stacked beneath the analog core. Without the band the same product is
440.94 × 191.05 = 84,241 µm². Neither number is a hard floor: the first treats a
placement choice as a rule, and the second ignores the reserve, which has to be
placed somewhere (≥ 8,646 µm² ringed).

**F2 — the cap array costs 41.9 % of the budget by rule alone.** At the fixed
4.0 µm plate the tile floor is 41,943 µm² before any dummy, spine, or
routing. Any layout at 0.1 mm² spends 42 % of it on 1,024 unit capacitors.

**F3 — the decode banks are 44.5 % whitespace in their own bbox.** The banks
are one 31.5 µm row of single-finger devices; 13,418 µm² of their bbox is not
drawn. The layout README records the lever (multi-finger folding) as blocked on
klayout-tools#261, which is closed (2026-08-02). Whether the klt this repo uses
carries it is **not verified here**.

**F4 — 20,653 µm² of the core's right column is empty and 37,322 µm² is the
band.** Both are placement, not rules. They are also the two regions the
`40cfeb8` record named as pinned (comparator placement) and declared out of
scope (SAR reserve placement).

## What ≈ 0.1 mm² would require (inferred arithmetic on measured inputs)

Mandatory pieces if every sub-block stays as drawn today:

| Piece | µm² |
|---|---|
| Union of sub-block bboxes | 85,596.6 |
| Ringed SAR reserve | 8,646.0 |
| 20 µm isolation strip over a 202 µm wide ring | 4,040 |
| Core guard-ring clearance for a ~440 × 190 core (2.9 µm × perimeter) | ~3,650 |
| **Sum, zero other whitespace** | **~101,900** |

So **no placement alone reaches 0.1 mm²**: even with every gap and margin at
zero the pieces as drawn add to about 101.9k µm². Getting under needs both a
perfect-packing floorplan and at least ~2k µm² (more, with any realistic
packing loss) removed from the pieces themselves. The candidates, with what
each puts at risk:

| Lever | Recoverable µm² (upper bound) | Rows at risk |
|---|---|---|
| Move the SAR reserve out of the full-width band into/beside the core | 28.6k–37.3k (block 114.5k–123.2k) | DR-0008/DR-0010 isolation re-argued; `sar_ctrl` macro re-P&R (it sits in a 199.21 × 40 µm box with zero margin). The two largest right-column holes (157 × 75 and 110 × 69 µm) are each smaller than the 202 × 43 µm ringed reserve at its current shape once a 20 µm gap is kept, so a new macro footprint is part of the cost |
| Fold the decode banks (multi-finger) | ≤ 13.4k, plus ≤ 2.4k of stubs | `comp.space.1` pitch, LVS device merge, bank parasitics and the settling rows |
| Trim the array edge margin | ≤ 7.4k | INL/DNL (PASS, stretch missed), common-centroid edge matching |
| Re-place the comparator/switch toward the banks | part of the 20.7k right column | `topp`/`topn` +190–250 µm of Metal1 (≈ 10 fF on 18.25 pF, ≈ 0.055 % gain error per the `40cfeb8` record); PEX re-run |
| Shrink the unit cap or tile pitch | 0 allowed | DR-0019 and the MIM rules; **not** considered, per #644 |

Upper bounds are not additive and none was drawn. The first two together are the
only pair whose upper bounds exceed the 51.8k needed; neither has been tried.
