## Why this record exists

This branch (`feature/issue-814`, PR #825) and `main` each re-attested T1
item 10 **independently** off the same predecessor record
(`20261113-152950-0ae70e77`), so the Supersedes chain forked:

- this branch re-attested for #814's `sim/README.md` cross-bundle link
  ([`20261115-050601-b99348ad`](20261115-050601-b99348ad.md), envelope pinning
  `sim/README.md` `ef8501f5…` but the **old** `ci.yml` `14b3f9ae…`);
- `main` re-attested twice for #804's CI-gate work
  ([`20261115-065154-ea92f5dc`](20261115-065154-ea92f5dc.md), then
  [`20261115-163917-5274163f`](20261115-163917-5274163f.md) when
  `check:lvs-requests` was wired into `check:ci` and the INVENTORY comment in
  `.github/workflows/ci.yml` moved again — envelope pinning the **new**
  `ci.yml` `086a222e…` but the base `sim/README.md` `18be0cb5…`).

Merging `origin/main` into this branch (Doctor conflict resolution; GitHub
reported the PR `CONFLICTING`) conflicted on exactly the four files those two
re-attests both moved: the envelope, `freshness.json`, and the two
current-record pointers. Neither side's envelope describes the merged tree,
which carries **both** edits — `sim/README.md` at `ef8501f5…` (this branch)
and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` at `086a222e…` (`main`). Resolution follows the
flow `signoff/README.md` § "Changing the verdict" prescribes and the check's
own failure message repeats: take `main`'s chain (the live one), re-attest
item 10 by hand over the merged tree, then `--regen`. This record supersedes
`main`'s tip and so **closes the fork**; `20261115-050601-b99348ad` remains a
side record of the branch's own re-attest, and its `sim/README.md` pin is the
one this merged envelope now carries forward.

**The `README.md` hash lag is the designed one** the previous two records
document: `README.md` is inventoried by item 10, but step 4 of
`signoff/README.md`'s "Changing the verdict" re-points its
`signoff:current-record` citation *after* the record id exists, so every mint
records the pre-re-point bytes. The `6fc0b23e…` -> `36266860…` move above is
the merged (pre-re-point) `README.md`, and this record's own re-point moves it
again for the next mint. `README.md` is exempt from `--check`'s envelope
re-hash and from `freshness.json`'s `artifacts` pins alike, for precisely this
reason.
