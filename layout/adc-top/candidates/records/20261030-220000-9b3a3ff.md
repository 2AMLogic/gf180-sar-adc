## Findings

1. **The reserve does not fit the right-column hole without overhang.** The
   top-right hole is 148.2 µm wide between its ring walls; a 202 µm ring plus
   the 20 µm gap and ring offset needs 226.5 µm. It fits vertically (about 50
   to 65 µm available, 42.8 needed), so the reserve must overhang the core's
   right edge.
2. **Best candidate is 127,759 µm² (top-right notch), −24,068 µm² (−15.9 %)
   against today's block.** It misses the issue's 114.5k to 123.2k target and
   stays a FAIL against the `< 0.1 mm²` row. The target assumed the reserve
   fits inside the core's bbox; it does not.
3. **Realising it requires an L-shaped analog guard ring**, which
   `geo.draw_guard_ring` (rectangular boxes only) and `_strap_guard_rings`
   (one straight digital-to-analog strap) do not support. That is a generator
   change plus full DRC/LVS/ERC/PEX regeneration, so no layout is committed.
4. **The `sar_ctrl` footprint (199.21 × 40.0 µm) is unchanged** in every
   candidate; no new P&R is implied.
5. Isolation: 20 µm ring-to-ring (24.46 µm to the nearest device bbox) in the
   notch candidates, same as today. DR-0008 is not relaxed.
6. Caveat: the script's comparator-top wall (89.3 µm) is the instance bbox top;
   record `20261030-210500-be1c350` uses 103.7 µm. The width result and the
   vertical fit (about 50 µm even with 103.7) are unaffected.
