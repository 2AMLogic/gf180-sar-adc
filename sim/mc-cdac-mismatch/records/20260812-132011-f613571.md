  **Negative control, read honestly**: the 3x-sigma defect (`σ_u = 1.5 %`)

  rows, but **not** on `inl_at_256_lsb_baseline` — the ±1 LSB baseline

  sigma degradation does not push INL's empirical yield below what a
  2000-sample negative-control draw can distinguish from 1.0 at 95%
  confidence. This is not a defect in the negative control; it is the
  honest, expected outcome of a loose limit against a moderate injected
  degradation, and is reported as `klt yield` itself reports it
  (per-measurement, not a single pass/fail for the whole campaign) — see
  `docs/cli/yield.md#negative-control`.
