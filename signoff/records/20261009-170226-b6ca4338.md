## Why this record exists

The routed `sar_ctrl` post-route STA baseline
(`design/sar-logic/flow/sar_ctrl/records/20260915-093934-7ab8971.mcu7t5v0.sta_postroute.md`)
was described in `sim/characterization-summary.md` and in the item-8 digital
envelope as timed from a "LEF/DEF-geometry RC estimate". That was wrong. The
`klt sta` script that produced those numbers has no `estimate_parasitics`,
`set_wire_rc` or `read_spef` step, so the baseline slack includes **no wire
parasitics** (unannotated, Liberty pin capacitance only). The correction is
recorded in the "Correction to how the baseline is described" section of
`design/sar-logic/flow/sar_ctrl/records/20261009-131918-bf22e71c.mcu7t5v0.sta_postroute_spef.md`
(issue #481, PR #483), which this change cites.

What changed:

- `sim/characterization-summary.md`: the digital partition's first disclosed
  limitation is reworded to "no wire parasitics (unannotated, Liberty pin
  capacitance only)", cites the correction, says the result is still not
  SPEF-annotated and still uses an ideal clock, and distinguishes the
  baseline from the later partially annotated SPEF experiment (#481), whose
  annotation was rejected at all five corners and whose slacks are not
  quoted.
- `signoff/evidence/characterization-summary.digital.json`: both incorrect
  strings are replaced, the "no SPEF" wording is qualified, and a #484
  `partial_updates[]` entry documents the scoped update. The analog envelope
  gets a matching entry noting a re-pin only.
- `reread_commit`/`reread_date` are **not** bumped in either envelope: no full
  row-by-row re-read was redone. Measured values, corners and the
  ideal-clock limitation are unchanged.
