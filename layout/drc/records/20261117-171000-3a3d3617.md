Semantics that decided the geometry (read from the deck source, then
confirmed by measurement): the gate-enclosure rules check `Poly2 ∩ Comp`
— a full-height gate stripe — enclosed by the implant **euclidianly**, so a
covering implant must clear the ACTIVE's top/bottom edges by the full 230;
the diffusion-enclosure rules check `Comp ∩ implant` enclosed by the implant
(160); the cross-spaces are measured to the opposite implant's DIFFUSION
(`Pplus ∩ Comp` / `Nplus ∩ Comp`), never implant-to-implant, so the
PMOS-Pplus-to-tap-Nplus boundary is decided by `TAP_ACTIVE_GAP` (400)
against 160 on both sides — 170 and 240 as drawn. Gates an implant does not
touch are exempt from the enclosure check (the deck's zero-overlap escape),
which is why the `Nplus` and `Pplus` regions never constrain each other's
devices.

## The new family this change activates: `comp.tap_distance.*` (DF.13/DF.14)

Drawing `Nplus` on NMOS S/D makes `NCOMP outside Nwell` non-empty for the
first time (and `Pplus` on PMOS S/D does the same for `PCOMP inside Nwell`),
so the 0.8.0 deck's latch-up tap-distance rules go from vacuous to live:
every n+ diffusion outside a well must sit within 20 µm of a p+ substrate
tie (DF.14_LV), and every p+ diffusion inside a well within 20 µm of an
n+ well tap (DF.13_LV). This block's substrate ties are the two guard rings
at the core's edge and its well taps are one strip per island, so the
interior reports: `adc_block` 84 / `adc_top` 67 / comparator 15 / leaf cells
1–8 (`psub`), plus 1 `nwell` marker on `adc_tgate_dum` (its ~80 µm-tall
dummy PMOS reaches below its single tap strip). Before this change both
rules were vacuous (`NCOMP` outside wells was empty; the 0.6.0 deck has
neither rule), which is why no pinned verdict moves — and why #652's 0.8.0
pin bump will inherit exactly this finding. The fix is a tap/tie lattice,
which #836's ratified ACs pin OUT of this issue (`well_tap_candidates == 25`,
`substrate_tie_candidates == 2`): tracked in
[#885](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-sar-adc/issues/885).
