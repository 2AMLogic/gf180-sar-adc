- **Note**: The behavioural CDAC in this deck is built FROM THE CONTROLLER'S OWN switch-driver outputs (each cell's bottom plate takes V_in, V_cm, V_REF or GND according to the sel_in/rel/sel_hi/sel_lo gate drives the DUT actually asserts), not from an independent model of what the controller should have done. That is what makes the closed loop a test rather than a tautology: a wrong direction, a wrong side, a cell that engages a trial early or never releases all move the top plate wrongly and produce a wrong code. Under DR-0014 the top plate is 2*V_cm - V_sampled + DAC, so the sampled input enters inverted -- which is why the controller inverts the comparator once at its boundary, and why a loop that got only ONE of those two inversions right would either diverge or emit the one's complement of every code.

It is evidence that the sequencing, the switch decode and the output register are functionally correct on every code in both input modes.
- **Note**: WHAT THE TWO-PHASE SAMPLE CHECK COVERS AND WHAT IT DOES NOT. tp_open_lead_ns is measured on the controller's two output edges, and it is the ordering claim. It is NOT evidence that the resulting charge transfer is correct at transistor level: the four things DR-0014's Consequences require -- the top-plate switch's own injection and its side-to-side mismatch, the bottom-plate legs' injection after that switch has opened, the fourth leg's effect on bit-trial settling, and second-order residue from C_par mismatch -- are all analog measurements on the transistor-level converter, and #61 owns them.
