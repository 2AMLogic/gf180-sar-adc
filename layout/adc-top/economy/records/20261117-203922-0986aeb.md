| before (no tap lattice) | 671.005 × 191.92 | 128 779.280 | 65 933.743 | 0.5120 | 62 845.5 |

**bbox +2 049.374 µm² (+1.59 %)**; block Y grew 2.88 µm (the per-row strip
stack — 500 nm `metal1.space` clearance + 400 nm Comp + 160 nm `Pplus`
overhang — paid once per stacked placed row: decode bank P, decode bank N,
and the comparator/switch band shift the same way inside their cells) and
block X grew 0.6 µm (the strip Metal1 bars reaching `RISER_W//2 + 80` past
their riser columns at the banks' right edge). Drawn area grew 834.0 µm² —
the strips' own Comp/Contact/Metal1 plus the extra well-tap strips — so
whitespace ROSE: the strips sit in field that was already empty.

**Against DR-0024's bound:** 130 828.654 µm² = 0.130829 mm² = **81.77 % of
the 0.16 mm² bound; headroom 29 171.346 µm².** (The figures DR-0035 and the
#836 economy record reported — 94.9 % and 80.49 % — described earlier
streams; the 80.49 % one is this record's `before` row.) DR-0024's bound is
not reopened; the lattice costs 1.28 percentage points of it.
