## Judgment

**Block kind**: mixed-signal top-level integration — two common-centroid
CDAC capacitor arrays (matched), two decode/switch banks, two top-plate
`V_cm` switches, a comparator (matched differential pair + guard-ring
isolation), and an explicitly reserved, deliberately undrawn SAR-logic
digital region (DR-0010: no 3.3 V standard-cell library on the open
gf180mcu PDK) — per `layout/adc-top/README.md`'s own composition table.
Rubric expectation for this kind: utilization 0.40–0.65 (economy-review
`SKILL.md` §3 table).

**Overall utilization 0.3516 sits below the mixed-signal floor (0.40)**,
close to but still under the "analog: isolation-heavy" range (0.30–0.50) and
the "analog: matched pairs" range (0.35–0.55) alone would fit — but the
block additionally contains a deliberately-reserved digital region, which
is why the mixed-signal comparison is the more apt one. Below-floor
utilization is a signal to look for concrete waste, not an automatic fail —
walked per the rubric below.

1. **CDAC arrays (`ADC_CDAC_ARRAY_N`/`_P`, utilization 0.802 each)** —
   tight, no finding. This is common-centroid unit-capacitor tiling at the
   DR-0019-ratified `C_u`; the DRM's own MiM tiling pitch (`MIMTM.1`/`.2`/
   `.3`, see README "The MiM stack") sets this floor, not a placement
   choice. The DR-0019 resize (#202) grew each array's area from 15,687.96
   to 24,669.60 µm² (+57.2%) while utilization stayed essentially unchanged
   (0.802 vs. the pre-resize proportional figure) — the growth tracked the
   unit cap, not slack. **Legitimate, matching-driven area.**

2. **Decode banks (`ADC_DECODE_BANK_N`/`_P`, utilization 0.446 each)** —
   within the rubric's expected range for switch/decode logic adjacent to
   matched analog structures; README documents this bank's width as set by
   the `comp.space.1` DRC floor (issue #67/#69), not a loose placement.
   **Legitimate.**

3. **SAR-logic reserved region (`largest_empty_regions` bbox
   `[-9.30,-73.10]`–`[195.61,-33.10]`, 7,714.2 µm², fill_fraction 0.941)** —
   this is DR-0010's deliberately-undrawn reserve (no transistor-level
   netlist exists to place; the area is reserved and ringed, per README's
   own composition table). Matches the block's known digital-reserve
   footprint. **Legitimate, not waste** — the rubric's own guidance that a
   named, spec-justified reserve is not the same as an unexplained gap.

4. **Right-side dead corridor — the review's one substantive finding.**
   `dead_margins_um.right = 154.03 µm` (the finer 8×6-grid walk `klt
   economy` uses for this field; see `docs/cli/economy.md` for the
   definition) is by far the largest of the four edges (left 0.0, bottom/top
   48.81 each) and is **not** explained by any guard-ring or matching
   structure of comparable width — the block's own guard ring is a single
   thin Comp/Contact/Metal1 ring (README "§2.4 guard rings"), not a 150 µm
   band. The coordinate evidence, cross-checked against
   `images/detail-right-corridor/overview.png` (visual confirmation: a
   roughly rectangular, near-full-height blank region bounded by the guard
   ring at top/right, crossed only by two or three thin Metal2/3/5 routing
   traces):
   - `[440.36,-1.70]`–`[592.43,23.05]` — 3,763.7 µm², **fill_fraction 1.000**
     (fully empty, no exceptions)
   - `[439.36,-8.52]`–`[592.43,-2.04]` — 991.0 µm², fill_fraction 0.999
   - `[437.36,24.63]`–`[592.43,31.11]` — 986.0 µm², fill_fraction 0.981
   - `[473.30,31.45]`–`[600.23,213.79]` — 17,374.2 µm², fill_fraction 0.751
     (the largest of the cluster; the 25% that IS covered is the
     `COMPARATOR` cell itself plus a couple of Metal2/3/5 straps threading
     through, visible in the detail render as the two/three vertical teal
     lines)

   These four regions total ≈23,115 µm² essentially fully empty, inside a
   roughly 165 µm-wide × 215 µm-tall envelope immediately right of the
   decode banks' actual right edge and around/beside the `COMPARATOR` cell.
   This is consistent with, and plausibly a direct descendant of, the
   already-documented "far rail-stitch column" mechanism in README ("issue
   #67": the block's right edge is derived from
   `max(bank_w, top.bbox().right) + 6 µm`, which reaches only as far as the
   comparator's own placement — a thin Metal1/2/3 trunk extension, not a
   claim that the whole reached span is filled). Nothing in the currently
   committed evidence explains why the reached span itself needs to be ~150
   µm wide rather than a routing-width corridor.

5. **`COMPARATOR`'s own internal utilization (0.086)** — below the rubric's
   0.25 "matched pairs" red-flag floor. README's own "Resistors, and why
   there are two comparator cells" section documents part of the cause: the
   two 150 kΩ load resistors are now drawn as ONE straight `ppolyf_u_1k`
   body each (`r_length=150 µm`), a **stated, LVS-driven deviation** from
   the prior two-segment common-centroid split (splitting would extract as
   two 75 kΩ devices in series, an LVS device-count mismatch against the
   schematic's one lumped device) — legitimate, not an oversight. But
   README's own text also already flags this as unresolved: *"A denser
   single-body layout (e.g. folding the 150 µm run, or narrowing `r_width`
   instead of doubling `r_length`) was not pursued in this increment; it is
   a candidate for a follow-up if the area cost … is not acceptable."* This
   review's numbers make that area cost concrete and, combined with finding
   4's adjacent dead corridor, actionable.

## Verdict

**revise**

The bulk of `adc_block`'s 0.18045 mm² — the CDAC arrays (DR-0019-driven,
tight at 0.802 utilization) and decode banks (DRC-floor-driven, 0.446) — is
legitimately spent and supports #198 ratifying a target at or near the
current drawn size for those structures. But finding 4 (the right-side dead
corridor, ~23,000 µm² essentially fully empty with no adjacent guard-ring or
matching rationale of comparable width) and finding 5 (the comparator's own
88.6%-empty local bbox, already flagged by README as an unpursued follow-up
candidate) are named, coordinate-level waste, not settled by DR-0019 or by
the #118 resistor-matching decision. Recovering even a fraction of finding
4's ~23,000 µm² (12.8% of the current 180,446 µm² total) would materially
change what #198's ratified number needs to be.

### Revision targets (numbered, for a follow-up layout-revision issue)

**R1 — right-side dead corridor.** Region `x ≈ 437–605 µm`, `y ≈ -12 to 214
µm` (see finding 4's four `largest_empty_regions` bboxes above) is >75–100%
empty with no adjacent guard-ring/matching structure at that width. Pull the
`COMPARATOR` placement and its rail-stitch trunk inward (leftward), or
re-derive the block's right-edge computation (`max(bank_w,
top.bbox().right) + 6 µm`, README "issue #67") so the reached span is sized
to the routing that actually needs it rather than to the comparator's raw
placement offset. Target: recover a meaningful fraction of the ~23,000 µm²
identified in finding 4 without touching the CDAC arrays, decode banks, or
the ratified `C_u`/DR-0019 geometry.

**R2 — comparator internal bbox.** `COMPARATOR` bbox 121.63 × 158.45 µm,
drawn only 1,648.0 µm² (8.55%). Investigate a denser single-body resistor
layout (folding the 150 µm run, or narrowing `r_width` instead of doubling
`r_length`, per README's own already-stated candidate) to shrink the cell's
own bounding box, independent of R1.

Both targets are additive with the SAR-logic reserve (finding 3, DR-0010,
untouched) and the CDAC/decode-bank geometry (findings 1–2, untouched) — a
revision here does not reopen the DR-0019 or #118 decisions.
