Two consequences for a reader of this deck: `v(tie_cmpo)` mid-rail is NOT issue #296's rejected soft comparator returning (the decision on `v(tie_cmpd)` is hard and always at a rail -- `probe_cmp_convergence.py --probe` prints both side by side), and `tie_code_deviation` is a check that the code lands adjacent to mid-scale, not a bit-wise reproducible number.
- **Note**: COMPUTATIONAL COST, MEASURED (issue #322): the chatter above is the dominant part of this loop's cost premium. DR-0029 records that saving as the strongest argument against its own decision and declines it anyway; budget this deck at issue #311's measured 233.7 core-s per point via sim/run_corners.py, not at `ok`'s ~132.
