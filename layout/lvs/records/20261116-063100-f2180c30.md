## The SB.10 halo probe (routed trade, measured here)

With the halo actually drawn (Poly2 = serpentine dilated 220 nm, fold gap
and column gap at the SB.10-forced 680 nm minimum, the drawn-area identity
re-solved so the marked region is still exactly 2 x (150000 x 1000) nm²):

| | committed | halo drawn |
|---|---|---|
| `klt extract` devices | 29 (`nfet` 15, `pfet` 12, `ppolyf_u_1k` 2) | **27** — no `ppolyf_u_1k` at all |
| nets / pins | 21 / 11 | 19 / 9 (`pop`/`pon` merged away) |
| contact components touching each marked wire | 2 | 1 |

The deck derives terminals from Poly2 minus the recognised body; the
SB.10-mandated band is unmarked Poly2 surrounding the body, merging both
leads into one contact region, and no two-terminal device forms. Full
reasoning and the deck-legal impossibility argument: the DRC record
referenced above. The halo tree was reverted; what lands here is the SB.2
fix only.
