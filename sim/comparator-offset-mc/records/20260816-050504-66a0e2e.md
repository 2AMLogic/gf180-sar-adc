  **\*Read the "fail" verdict correctly: it is a sample-size artifact of
  this deck's own N = 150-per-corner design choice, not a margin
  violation.** All three measurements have **zero samples outside their
  limits** and `sigma_to_spec` of 5–63σ — nowhere near the 3σ target. `klt
  yield`'s own rule (`docs/cli/yield.md#never-a-bare-point-estimate`) is
  that a run with zero observed failures reports its yield as bounded only
  from below, at the stated confidence — here, "at least 97.5707 % at 95 %
  confidence, N = 150" — and that lower bound (0.975707) does not clear the
  declared 3σ `target_yield` (0.9973002…) at 95 % confidence, so the
  measurement is correctly reported `fail` even though the design itself is
  nowhere close to the limit. `sample_size.required_n_for_target` in the
  JSON confirms it: **N ≥ 1365** would support the claim at this same
  observed pass rate; N = 150 was sized for this deck's *corner-matrix* wall
  time (45 points × 150 draws, DR-0007's verification budget — see the
  source record's own "Sigma level" note), not for a single-corner `klt
  yield`-precision claim. This is the same "insufficient N" outcome the
  worked example in `docs/cli/yield.md` itself demonstrates at N = 300 — it
  is `klt yield` doing exactly its job (refusing a bare point estimate),
  not a design defect.

  **CMRR extrapolation, stated explicitly per `spec/testbench-suite-memo.md`
  §10**: the ±50 µV limit above is the ratified ≥ 60 dB target **linearly
  extrapolated down** from the ratified condition's ±100 mV common-mode band
  to this campaign's own measured ±50 mV band: `100 mV / 10^(60/20) =
  100 µV` at the ratified band, and because CMRR is a *ratio* — a
  first-order (linear) common-mode sensitivity extrapolates unchanged, only
  a second-order term would differ (§10) — the equivalent absolute limit at
  the HALVED ±50 mV band is likewise halved, `100 mV/2 / 10^(60/20) =
  50 µV`. Testing these real ±50 mV samples against 50 µV is therefore
  *identically* the same pass/fail statement §10 makes about the ratified
  ±100 mV row, not a loosened one. **A direct ±100 mV measurement is not
  performed here** (§10's own stated limitation, carried forward, not
  closed).

  The systematic figure is close to the prior citation (118.2 vs 117.5 dB).
  **The 3σ-mismatch figure is materially different (85.6 vs 99.0 dB) — real,
  not a discrepancy to paper over.** The prior citation's source record
  (`20260801-035221-90d7e67.md`) has 7 of 45 corners **errored**
  (`ss_-40c_*`: ngspice exit −9; `sf_-40c_*`: timeout) — it never measured
  `ss_-40c_3.63v`, the exact corner this clean, complete 45/45-point re-run
  finds is the true worst case. The prior 99.0 dB was the worst **of the
  corners that happened to converge**, not the worst of the grid. **Both
  figures still clear the ratified ≥ 60 dB target by a large margin** (85.6
  dB is 25.6 dB above baseline, 20.6 dB above the ≥ 65 dB stretch) — this is
  a correction to a previously-narrower citation, not a new finding that
  threatens the row.

  **A single-trial outlier explains `ss_-40c_3.63v`'s elevated `sig_dvos_*`,
  now visible for the first time.** Of `ss_-40c_3.63v`'s 150 draws, trial 47
  alone produces `ddc = −9.503 µV` / `dde = −10.407 µV`, roughly 20× the
  typical ±0.5 µV scatter of the other 149 draws (see
  `cmrr_dvos_dn_uv_worst_corner`'s `normality: rejected` above — Anderson–
  Darling `A2* = 33.57` against the `0.787` critical value, driven by this
  one heavy tail, not a systematically shifted population). Both `ddc` and
  `dde` spike on the *same* trial because they share the `voa` term
  (`ddc = voc − voa`, `dde = voe − voa`); trial 47's own `voa = −3.296e-3` V
  is an ordinary ~2.6σ offset draw (`sig_vos_mv` at this corner is
  ≈ 1.281 mV) that happens to coincide with a locally smaller per-draw gain
  (`ava`), which the `−voa/ava` construction of `voc`/`voe` — and hence the
  `ddc`/`dde` differences — amplifies. This is a real, reproducible
  sensitivity of the CM-step *delta* to individual mismatch draws at this
  corner, not a convergence failure (`av_sigma_pct` and `sig_rpair_uv` both
  read normally for `ss_-40c_3.63v` in the source record — its own
  per-corner self-checks pass), and it still clears the ±50 µV limit by
  ≈ 5×. Before this record's `print` addition this behaviour was invisible —
  only the aggregate `sig_dvos_*` value was ever recorded, with no way to
  see that one trial, not the whole corner, drove it.
