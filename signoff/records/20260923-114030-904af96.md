## What moved in the verdict

```
block: gf180-sar-adc  kind: mixed-signal
tier: none
T1: 7/22 items met        (unchanged)
```

**No row changed.** Every entry in `items[]` is identical to the superseded
record, field for field. Checked mechanically, not by reading:

```python
rows = lambda d: {(i["id"], i.get("partition", "")): (i["status"], i.get("reason"))
                  for i in d["items"]}
rows(old) == rows(new)        # True
old["t1_met_count"], new["t1_met_count"]   # (7, 7)
old["tier"], new["tier"]                   # (None, None)
```

That is the intended outcome and it is worth stating as a claim rather than an
absence. T1 item 8 asks whether a current, aggregated characterization report
**exists and has been re-read** — not whether the design passes. This change
re-derived six of that report's rows from fresh evidence and moved **no
verdict in either direction**: every passing row still passes, and ENOB, SFDR
and Area still FAIL, at the same worst corners they failed at before. A
re-extraction that had flipped a row would have been the finding; it did not,
and that null is the finding instead.

The three failing rows the item-8 envelopes name are re-quoted to the new
figures — ENOB 8.857 → **8.855** bits, SFDR 60.40 → **60.41** dB, Area
0.150536 → **0.151827** mm² — because a wrapper that names stale numbers is
the exact defect `signoff/evidence/README.md` exists to prevent. None of the
three crosses its threshold; all three were failing before and are failing
now.
