## Why this record exists

`20261020-213648-75c6b72c`) each minted a signoff record from a tree without the
other's pinned-file change. Merging `main` into #611 conflicted in the generated
current-record pointers (`README.md`, `signoff/README.md`) and in
`signoff/freshness.json`'s `report` block. Neither side of those conflicts was
hand-picked as the answer: the pointers were taken from `main`, and the verdict
was re-derived on the merged tree.
