## Rescoped out during the campaign

The **63-point `adc-power` resubmission** from the 2026-11-04 capacity park
was dropped (Curator, 2026-11-22): all 17 historical `c6d73a3` `adc-power`
records are 27-point (`tt/ss/ff`) grids, and `adc-power`'s default `cdac`
corner set holds MOS process at `tt`, so a 63-point run fails the
manifest's own `p_cmp_f050_uw` process-axis floor (0.06 % vs 2 %) — an
unintended default-corner-set expansion, not a like-for-like re-qualification.
`adc-power` schematic scope is complete at 27 points.
