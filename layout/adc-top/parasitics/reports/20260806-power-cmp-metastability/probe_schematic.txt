Uniform profile => top-4-of-32 share is 12.5 %. A materially larger
share localises the excess to a few bit trials (decision-limited);
~12.5 % with an elevated mean is a static/bias shift instead.

Bit cycle b spans [b*CLK_PERIOD_NS, (b+1)*CLK_PERIOD_NS) from the
conversion start. Trial i decides in bit cycle 3+i (i = 1..10), so
bit cycles 0-3 are the ACQUISITION phases, not bit trials.
