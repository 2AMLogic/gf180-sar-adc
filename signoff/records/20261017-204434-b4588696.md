## Why this record exists

`6.digital` read `unmet` / `no_evidence`. Item 6 asks a block whose spec rows are
not statistical to "say so explicitly rather than omit the item", and since
klayout-tools#2865 that has a graded form: a recorded **not applicable**, valid
only when the cited spec is ratified, a committed statement gives every spec row
a class and a reason it is not statistical, and both are bound by content hash.
This change cites that form for the digital partition.

## What moved in the verdict

## What this record does NOT assert

- That the digital partition passes anything. Timing (item 5), the rung-2
  gate-level replay, LVS (item 4) and the SPEF-annotated STA are all as before;
  the statement classifies the rows, it does not grade them.
- That the whole ADC has no statistical row. It has several (ENOB, INL/DNL,
  offset, gain, CMRR); they are the analog partition's, and `6.analog` cites a
  `klt yield` report for the one it covers. The analog partition may never use
  this form.
- That Power and Area, which `sar_ctrl` contributes to, are digital rows. They
  are whole-block totals and were left out of the projection.
- That the aperture-jitter figure in the Clock row is a digital quantity. It is
  an rms figure for the external clock source and was omitted from the Clock
  row (`omitted_from_projection`); the row quotes only the frequency clause.
- Anything about a pin choice beyond "the first build this repo could find that
  grades the outcome".
