**bbox +14.394 µm² (+0.011 %)**; block X grew 0.075 µm — the outermost
implant overhang past the rightmost decode-bank active (bank width
440.94 → 441.17 µm in `layout/adc-top/area.json`, i.e. the full 0.23 µm

dominated the bbox). The drawn area grew 2 211.6 µm² — the implant shapes
themselves, which blanket diffusion that was already drawn, so whitespace

**Against DR-0024's bound:** 128 779.280 µm² = 0.128779 mm² = **80.49 % of
the 0.16 mm² bound; headroom 31 220.720 µm².** (The figure DR-0035 recorded —
151 827.342 µm², 94.9 %, headroom 8 172.658 µm² — described the pre-DR-0043
full-band reserve and was superseded by the top-right-notch move, issue
#667/DR-0043, before this change.) DR-0024's bound is not reopened; the
growth fits the headroom by three orders of magnitude.
