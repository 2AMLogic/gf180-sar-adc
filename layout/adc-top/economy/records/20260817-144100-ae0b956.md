## What moved, against R1 and R2

**R2 (comparator internal bbox).** `COMPARATOR` is 116.93 × 47.05 µm
(5,501.6 µm²), was 121.63 × 158.45 µm (19,272.3 µm²) — **−71.5 %**, local
utilization 0.086 → 0.279. Each 150 kΩ `ppolyf_u_1k` load body is now a
four-leg serpentine instead of a 150 µm straight run, so the resistor no
longer sets the cell's height at all (the folded body stands 37.75 µm above
the row baseline, under the 40 µm the preamp's own `w=40u` input pair
already occupies). It is still **one connected polygon and one extracted
device per resistor**, at exactly 150,000 Ω — the split that would have
re-introduced #118's device-count mismatch was not used. 0.279 clears the
0.25 "matched pairs" red-flag floor the baseline's finding 5 measured this
cell against; it did not before.

**R1 (right-side dead corridor).** The baseline named four
`largest_empty_regions` totalling ≈23,115 µm². Matched against this run:

The 17,374 µm² region — the largest of the cluster, and the one whose
"25 % that IS covered is the `COMPARATOR` cell itself" the baseline called
out — falls by 13,607 µm². It is the same recovery R2 delivers, seen from
the block's side: that region *was* the comparator's own envelope. The
DR-0010 SAR-logic reserve (`[-9.30,-73.10]`–`[194.04,-33.10]`, 7,655.3 µm²,
fill 0.941) is unchanged in kind and remains legitimate per the baseline's
finding 3.

**`dead_margins_um.right` barely moves (154.03 → 152.86), and that is a
property of the metric, not a null result.** `dead_margins_um` is walked on
a fixed 8 × 6 grid of the block's *own* bbox and is therefore quantised to
whole grid bands: 154.03 µm was exactly 2 of 8 columns of a 616.13 µm-wide
block, and 152.86 µm is exactly 2 of 8 columns of a 611.43 µm-wide one — the
same *proportion* (25.0 %) of a smaller block. It cannot fall below 2
columns until the rightmost 76.4 µm band's mean coverage passes 20 %, which
the comparator row alone cannot reach at any fold count. The absolute
recovery shows up in `bbox_area_um2`, `utilization`, and the empty-region
table above; `dead_margins_um.top` going 48.81 → **0.0** is the one edge
where the grid does record the change, and it records it because the block's
top edge is now the CDAC arrays' own, not the comparator's.

## Judgment

Overall utilization is **0.4094**, inside the economy-review rubric's
0.40–0.65 band for a mixed-signal block for the first time (the baseline's
0.3516 sat below the floor and is what triggered the walk that produced R1
and R2). Both named revision targets moved, by the amounts tabulated above,
without touching the CDAC arrays, the ratified `C_u`, the decode banks'
`comp.space.1`-floored pitch, or the DR-0010 reserve.

**This record does not issue a verdict.** It is the "quantify the
recovery" half of issue #215; whether 0.15365 mm² is the number to ratify
is #198's decision, and a fresh `economy-review` verdict — if one is wanted
on the new geometry — is its own pass.

## What was NOT re-measured or re-reviewed

- **Findings 1–3 of the baseline** (CDAC arrays, decode banks, SAR-logic
  reserve) — carried forward unchanged; the per-cell table above shows their
  numbers are unchanged, so re-arguing them here would add nothing.
- **Sub-die floorplan alternatives** (relocating the comparator, reshaping
  the 32 × 16 array) — still out of scope, as the baseline recorded.
- **The remaining ≈9,330 µm² of the R1 cluster.** Three of the four regions
  (the ≈5,560 µm² band at `y ≈ -8.5..31` right of the decode banks' device
  rows) are the corridor the banks' own rail trunks reach across to the far
  stitch column, and shortening that reach is a floorplan question (where
  the comparator's supply straps to the banks), not a cell-geometry one.
  Named here so it is not mistaken for un-noticed slack.
